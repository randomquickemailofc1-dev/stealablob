      How it works

      • The same maths as the game: the egg picks a rarity by its odds, then a random blob of that rarity (or a blob from its own list), then rolls for a mutation, rarest first.
      • Normal luck only: in the game, Egg Luck, friends in your server, Galaxy Hour and the Mutation of the Day make rare pulls more common.
      • 🍀 The Lucky Meter works like the game's: after enough hatches without the egg's rarest pull, the next one is guaranteed.
      • All odds for every egg are on the value list.
