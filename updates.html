Steal a Blob Updates

What's new in the game, newest first. A new blob drops every Friday; codes for big updates are on the codes page.

  1. Oct 26, 2026

    Milestones, time played and an ideas box

    • 🏅 Milestones for time played, steals, rebirths, eggs hatched, merges, blobs bought and slaps: reach one and the gems land automatically (Inventory → STATS shows your progress).
    • ⏱️ Time played pays extra: free Golden and Cosmic Eggs, the ⏱️ Dedicated title at 24 hours and ⏳ Blob Veteran at 100 hours. New lobby leaderboard: most time played.
    • 💡 An IDEAS box in the game (MORE → IDEAS): send ideas and bugs straight to our Discord.
    • 😈 Bandits can't drain an idle base anymore: at most one kidnap every 30 minutes, never while you're standing still.
    • 🧭 TAKE ME THERE: on steal goals, one tap walks you to the blob.
  2. Oct 25, 2026

    Supply drops, blackouts, decoys and thief ranks

    • 📦 Supply drops: a crate lands next to the Blob Road every few minutes. The first player to open it wins gems, cash, a 2x boost or a Golden Egg.
    • 🌑 Blackouts: about twice an hour all locks go down for 60 seconds (your lock picks up again afterwards). New players are never hit.
    • 🎭 Decoy blobs in the Gem Shop: a fake of your best blob that splats the thief who grabs it.
    • 🏅 Weekly thief ranks from Bronze to Diamond, with an icon on your head tag and gem rewards.
  3. Oct 25, 2026

    HARD challenges + Heist Mode

    • 🔥 A new HARD tab in 📜 Quests: from Rebirth 2, two tough challenges every day for gems, cash and a Golden Egg. Finish 10 for the 🔥 Daredevil title.
    • 😈 Heist Mode (opt-in from level 5): twice the thieves, they keep blobs at any rebirth, angry bosses. In return: +25% income while you're home and 2x cash for slapping thieves.
    • 🛡️ A new lobby leaderboard for the best Bandit Buster streak (thieves stopped on your own base in a row).
    • 👯 MERGE ALL in the Inventory merges every twin at once, all the way to ★★★.
  4. Oct 25, 2026

    Inventory 2.0 + bandits play for keeps

    • 🎒 A new Inventory: real 3D blobs, sort by value, income or rarity, ⚡ PLACE BEST and 🧹 SELL JUNK buttons, and a 👯 MERGE badge on twins.
    • 😈 From Rebirth 2, a bandit thief that gets home keeps your blob! Only you can steal it back from its hideout, within 5 minutes.
    • 🔥 More thieves the more you rebirth, and angrier bandit bosses from Rebirth 3.
    • 💸 2x boosts no longer multiply each other (the 2x Cash pass still does), and rebirth 4+ costs more.
    • 🛒 New players get a big BUY button for their first blob.
  5. Oct 25, 2026

    Blob Options: protect and move your blobs

    • Walk up to one of your blobs and press OPTIONS (Q): ⭐ protect it so it can't be sold by accident, 🔀 move it to another pedestal (upper floors are farther from thieves), store it, reroll it in the Mutation Machine or mark it for trade.
    • The Merge and Sell buttons on a blob no longer sit on top of each other: they stack neatly.
  6. Oct 25, 2026

    A free Golden Egg for linking Discord

    • Link your Roblox account in our Discord with /verify and get a free Golden Egg in the game (once per account). It shows up next time you play: open it from the 🥚 button.
  7. Oct 25, 2026

    Friends luck, a community goal and a cleaner phone screen

    • Phones: one event banner at a time (boosts take turns), the PLAYERS list folds away (tap + to open) and fewer buttons on screen (Spin and Gear are in MORE).
    • Friends luck: play with Roblox friends in your server for up to +25% egg luck.
    • Weekly community goal: every steal in every server counts. Reach it by Saturday and everyone gets 2x luck all weekend.
    • Daily rewards calendar with a streak shield: miss one day and the shield saves your streak (once every 7 days).
    • Got robbed right before you left? Next time you join you see who did it, with a button to chase them.
    • Day 2, Day 3 and Day 7 goals with eggs and titles when you come back.
    • Weekend Steal Contest: Friday 16:00 UTC to Monday. Steal the most and the top 3 get a title.
    • Clip of the Week: the winner of the Discord clip contest gets a title in game.
    • New players get their popups spaced out, and badge popups no longer show up black on phones.
    • New players: your first blob now rides up to you on the Blob Road, saved just for you. One tap and it's in your base.
    • After your first steal you're asked once to join the group: +10% income forever and a free Golden Egg.
  8. Oct 24, 2026

    First-day goals and a smoother start

    • First-day goals: a short chain of goals with eggs, gems and titles for your first half hour.
    • The tutorial now points you at a blob you can actually afford.
    • Cleaner chat tags: one tag per player.
    • Teleporting in the middle of a trade or a steal is blocked, so nothing gets lost.

  10. Oct 24, 2026

    Mutation Machine, Galaxy Hour and the Mutation Index

    • Mutation Machine (next to the Blob Factory): spend gems to reroll a blob's mutation. It never lands on no mutation, you choose KEEP or TAKE, and the 10th reroll of a blob is Diamond or better. Odds are shown before you pay.
    • Chase the thief: if someone robs you and leaves the server, a CHASE button follows them to their new server for revenge.
    • GALAXY HOUR: every Sunday at 17:00 UTC the Galaxy mutation is 5× likelier for an hour (1 in 500 → 1 in 100).
    • Mutation of the Day: one mutation is 2× likelier each day.
    • Friend boost: play with a Roblox friend for 5 minutes and you both get 2× Galaxy on eggs for 30 minutes.
    • Mutation Index: collect every blob in every mutation (INDEX → 🧬). Milestones give Golden and Cosmic Eggs, gems and 4 new titles.
    • A "NEW MUTATION FOUND" toast the first time you get a blob in a new mutation.
    • The Blob Factory's NOW MAKING screen flashes ⚠ RARE ⚠ with sparkles, beacons and an alarm when a Mythic-or-better blob or a rare mutation spawns.
    • 🧬 NEW badges in trades and Trading Plaza booths show the combos you don't have yet.
    • A new lobby leaderboard: Top Mutations.
