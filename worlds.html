Questions

How many worlds are in Steal a Blob?

7: Candy Cove, Lava Land, Frost Peaks, Cosmic Rift, Coral Reef, Mushroom Forest and Desert Pyramids. Above the lobby there are also 3 sky islands: Sky Island, Rainbow Isle and Galaxy Isle.

How do you unlock Candy Cove?

Open 🌍 WORLDS in game. Candy Cove needs level 3 and $100K (paid once).

What is the last world in Steal a Blob?

Desert Pyramids: it needs level 48, 15 rebirths and $56Qa.

What is the hardest boss in Steal a Blob?

The Pharaoh Gloop in Desert Pyramids: 22,000 health, +12,500 for every extra player. It can drop Pharaoh Gloop Jr (1 in 125).
