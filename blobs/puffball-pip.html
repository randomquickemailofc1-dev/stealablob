Questions

How much is Puffball Pip worth in Steal a Blob?

Puffball Pip is worth $500B ($500,000,000,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Puffball Pip is worth $75T. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Puffball Pip?

Puffball Pip comes from: Spore Egg (46%).

How much money does Puffball Pip make?

$5.5B per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Puffball Pip makes $825B per second.
