Questions

How much is Golden Goo God worth in Steal a Blob?

Golden Goo God is worth $20M ($20,000,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Golden Goo God is worth $3B. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Golden Goo God?

Golden Goo God comes from: Blob Road (1 in 12,101), Sky Road (1 in 5,169), Rainbow Road (1 in 3,824), Galaxy Road (1 in 3,076) or Cosmic Egg (1 in 60).

How much money does Golden Goo God make?

$170K per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Golden Goo God makes $25.5M per second.
