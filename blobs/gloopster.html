Questions

How much is Gloopster worth in Steal a Blob?

Gloopster is worth $2.5K ($2,500) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Gloopster is worth $375K. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Gloopster?

Gloopster comes from: Blob Road (1 in 61), Sky Road (3.9%), Rainbow Road (5.2%), Galaxy Road (6.5%), Basic Egg (1 in 55) or Golden Egg (7.5%).

How much money does Gloopster make?

$50 per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Gloopster makes $7.5K per second.
