Questions

How much is Starlight Squish worth in Steal a Blob?

Starlight Squish is worth $4M ($4,000,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Starlight Squish is worth $600M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Starlight Squish?

Starlight Squish comes from: Blob Road (1 in 8,067), Sky Road (1 in 1,149), Rainbow Road (1 in 255) or Galaxy Road (1 in 146).

How much money does Starlight Squish make?

$45K per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Starlight Squish makes $6.75M per second.
