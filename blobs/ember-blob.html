Questions

How much is Ember Blob worth in Steal a Blob?

Ember Blob is worth $1.6M ($1,600,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Ember Blob is worth $240M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Ember Blob?

Ember Blob comes from: Lava Egg (60%).

How much money does Ember Blob make?

$20K per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Ember Blob makes $3M per second.
