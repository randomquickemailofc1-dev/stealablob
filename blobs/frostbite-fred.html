Questions

How much is Frostbite Fred worth in Steal a Blob?

Frostbite Fred is worth $25K ($25,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Frostbite Fred is worth $3.75M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Frostbite Fred?

Frostbite Fred comes from: Blob Road (1 in 202), Sky Road (1 in 86), Rainbow Road (1 in 64), Galaxy Road (1 in 51), Basic Egg (1 in 600) or Golden Egg (6.7%).

How much money does Frostbite Fred make?

$380 per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Frostbite Fred makes $57K per second.
