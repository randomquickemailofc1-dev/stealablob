Questions

How much is Moon Mochi worth in Steal a Blob?

Moon Mochi is worth $6.5M ($6,500,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Moon Mochi is worth $975M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Moon Mochi?

Moon Mochi comes from: Blob Road (1 in 8,067), Sky Road (1 in 1,149), Rainbow Road (1 in 255), Galaxy Road (1 in 146) or Blob Pass.

How much money does Moon Mochi make?

$70K per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Moon Mochi makes $10.5M per second.
