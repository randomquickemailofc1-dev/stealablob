Questions

How much is Crystal Gloop worth in Steal a Blob?

Crystal Gloop is worth $32K ($32,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Crystal Gloop is worth $4.8M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Crystal Gloop?

Crystal Gloop comes from: Blob Road (1 in 202), Sky Road (1 in 86), Rainbow Road (1 in 64), Galaxy Road (1 in 51), Basic Egg (1 in 600) or Golden Egg (6.7%).

How much money does Crystal Gloop make?

$470 per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Crystal Gloop makes $70.5K per second.
