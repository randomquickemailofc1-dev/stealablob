Questions

How much is Captain Splat worth in Steal a Blob?

Captain Splat is worth $260K ($260,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Captain Splat is worth $39M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Captain Splat?

Captain Splat comes from: Blob Road (1 in 630), Sky Road (1 in 269), Rainbow Road (1 in 199), Galaxy Road (1 in 160), Golden Egg (2.8%) or Cosmic Egg (11%).

How much money does Captain Splat make?

$3.1K per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Captain Splat makes $465K per second.
