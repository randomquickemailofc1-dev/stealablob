Questions

How much is Fairy Fizz worth in Steal a Blob?

Fairy Fizz is worth $20K ($20,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Fairy Fizz is worth $3M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Fairy Fizz?

Fairy Fizz comes from: Blob Road (1 in 202), Sky Road (1 in 86), Rainbow Road (1 in 64), Galaxy Road (1 in 51), Basic Egg (1 in 600) or Golden Egg (6.7%).

How much money does Fairy Fizz make?

$310 per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Fairy Fizz makes $46.5K per second.
