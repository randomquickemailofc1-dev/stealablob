Questions

How much is Star Slime worth in Steal a Blob?

Star Slime is worth $680M ($680,000,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Star Slime is worth $102B. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Star Slime?

Star Slime comes from: Void Egg (60%).

How much money does Star Slime make?

$8M per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Star Slime makes $1.2B per second.
