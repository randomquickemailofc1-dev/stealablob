Questions

How much is Prism Pudding worth in Steal a Blob?

Prism Pudding is worth $1.6M ($1,600,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Prism Pudding is worth $240M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Prism Pudding?

Prism Pudding comes from: Blob Road (1 in 2,521), Sky Road (1 in 1,077), Rainbow Road (1 in 797), Galaxy Road (1 in 641), Golden Egg (1 in 400) or Cosmic Egg (10%).

How much money does Prism Pudding make?

$16K per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Prism Pudding makes $2.4M per second.
