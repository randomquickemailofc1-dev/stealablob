Questions

How much is Mummy Mush worth in Steal a Blob?

Mummy Mush is worth $260K ($260,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Mummy Mush is worth $39M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Mummy Mush?

Mummy Mush comes from: Limited, Halloween (1 in 1,146) or Spooky Night (1 in 66). It's a LIMITED blob: it only spawns during its season.

How much money does Mummy Mush make?

$3K per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Mummy Mush makes $450K per second.
