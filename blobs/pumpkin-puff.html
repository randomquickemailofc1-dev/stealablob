Questions

How much is Pumpkin Puff worth in Steal a Blob?

Pumpkin Puff is worth $25K ($25,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Pumpkin Puff is worth $3.75M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Pumpkin Puff?

Pumpkin Puff comes from: Limited, Halloween (1 in 315) or Spooky Night (5.5%). It's a LIMITED blob: it only spawns during its season.

How much money does Pumpkin Puff make?

$380 per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Pumpkin Puff makes $57K per second.
