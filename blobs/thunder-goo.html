Questions

How much is Thunder Goo worth in Steal a Blob?

Thunder Goo is worth $40K ($40,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Thunder Goo is worth $6M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Thunder Goo?

Thunder Goo comes from: Blob Road (1 in 202), Sky Road (1 in 86), Rainbow Road (1 in 64), Galaxy Road (1 in 51), Basic Egg (1 in 600) or Golden Egg (6.7%).

How much money does Thunder Goo make?

$580 per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Thunder Goo makes $87K per second.
