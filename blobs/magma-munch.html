How much is Magma Munch worth in Steal a Blob?

Magma Munch is worth $2.6M ($2,600,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Magma Munch is worth $390M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How much money does Magma Munch make?

$32K per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Magma Munch makes $4.8M per second.
