Questions

How much is Comet Cruller worth in Steal a Blob?

Comet Cruller is worth $12M ($12,000,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Comet Cruller is worth $1.8B. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Comet Cruller?

Comet Cruller comes from: Blob Road (1 in 8,067), Sky Road (1 in 1,149), Rainbow Road (1 in 255) or Galaxy Road (1 in 146).

How much money does Comet Cruller make?

$125K per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Comet Cruller makes $18.7M per second.
