How much is Toadstool Tot worth in Steal a Blob?

Toadstool Tot is worth $850B ($850,000,000,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Toadstool Tot is worth $127T. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Toadstool Tot?

Toadstool Tot comes from: Spore Egg (28%).

How much money does Toadstool Tot make?

$9.19B per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Toadstool Tot makes $1.38T per second.
