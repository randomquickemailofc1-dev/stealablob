Questions

How much is Nerdy Gel worth in Steal a Blob?

Nerdy Gel is worth $1.5K ($1,500) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Nerdy Gel is worth $225K. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Nerdy Gel?

Nerdy Gel comes from: Blob Road (5.2%) or Basic Egg (7.6%).

How much money does Nerdy Gel make?

$34 per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Nerdy Gel makes $5.09K per second.
