Questions

How much is Nebula Nugget worth in Steal a Blob?

Nebula Nugget is worth $160K ($160,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Nebula Nugget is worth $24M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Nebula Nugget?

Nebula Nugget comes from: Blob Road (1 in 630), Sky Road (1 in 269), Rainbow Road (1 in 199), Galaxy Road (1 in 160), Golden Egg (2.8%) or Cosmic Egg (11%).

How much money does Nebula Nugget make?

$2K per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Nebula Nugget makes $300K per second.
