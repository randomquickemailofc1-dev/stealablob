Questions

How much is Cloud Puff worth in Steal a Blob?

Cloud Puff is worth $10K ($10,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Cloud Puff is worth $1.5M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Cloud Puff?

Cloud Puff comes from: Blob Road (1 in 61), Sky Road (3.9%), Rainbow Road (5.2%), Galaxy Road (6.5%), Basic Egg (1 in 55) or Golden Egg (7.5%).

How much money does Cloud Puff make?

$150 per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Cloud Puff makes $22.5K per second.
