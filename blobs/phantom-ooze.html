Questions

How much is Phantom Ooze worth in Steal a Blob?

Phantom Ooze is worth $1.1M ($1,100,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Phantom Ooze is worth $165M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Phantom Ooze?

Phantom Ooze comes from: Blob Road (1 in 2,521), Sky Road (1 in 1,077), Rainbow Road (1 in 797), Galaxy Road (1 in 641), Golden Egg (1 in 400) or Cosmic Egg (10%).

How much money does Phantom Ooze make?

$12K per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Phantom Ooze makes $1.8M per second.
