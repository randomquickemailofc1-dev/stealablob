Questions

How much is Puddle Blob worth in Steal a Blob?

Puddle Blob is worth $25 ($25) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Puddle Blob is worth $3.75K. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Puddle Blob?

Puddle Blob comes from: Blob Road (15%) or Basic Egg (12%).

How much money does Puddle Blob make?

$1 per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Puddle Blob makes $150 per second.
