Questions

How much is Mossy worth in Steal a Blob?

Mossy is worth $120 ($120) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Mossy is worth $18K. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Mossy?

Mossy comes from: Blob Road (15%) or Basic Egg (12%).

How much money does Mossy make?

$4 per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Mossy makes $600 per second.
