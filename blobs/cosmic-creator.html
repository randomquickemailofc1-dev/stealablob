Questions

How much is Cosmic Creator worth in Steal a Blob?

Cosmic Creator is worth $10B ($10,000,000,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Cosmic Creator is worth $1.5T. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Cosmic Creator?

Cosmic Creator comes from: Void Egg (2%).

How much money does Cosmic Creator make?

$120M per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Cosmic Creator makes $18B per second.
