Questions

How much is Galaxy Gulp worth in Steal a Blob?

Galaxy Gulp is worth $2.5M ($2,500,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Galaxy Gulp is worth $375M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Galaxy Gulp?

Galaxy Gulp comes from: Blob Road (1 in 2,521), Sky Road (1 in 1,077), Rainbow Road (1 in 797), Galaxy Road (1 in 641), Golden Egg (1 in 400) or Cosmic Egg (10%).

How much money does Galaxy Gulp make?

$24K per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Galaxy Gulp makes $3.6M per second.
