Questions

How much is Neon Noodle worth in Steal a Blob?

Neon Noodle is worth $5M ($5,000,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Neon Noodle is worth $750M. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Neon Noodle?

Neon Noodle comes from: Exclusive Egg (30%) or Blob Pass.

How much money does Neon Noodle make?

$48K per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Neon Noodle makes $7.2M per second.
