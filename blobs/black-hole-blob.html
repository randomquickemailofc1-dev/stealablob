How much is Black Hole Blob worth in Steal a Blob?

Black Hole Blob is worth $3B ($3,000,000,000) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Black Hole Blob is worth $450B. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Black Hole Blob?

Black Hole Blob comes from: Void Egg (10%).

How much money does Black Hole Blob make?

$35M per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Black Hole Blob makes $5.25B per second.
