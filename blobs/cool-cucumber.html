Questions

How much is Cool Cucumber worth in Steal a Blob?

Cool Cucumber is worth $3.5K ($3,500) as a normal 1-star blob. Mutations and stars multiply that: a Galaxy ★★★ Cool Cucumber is worth $525K. Selling one gives back 50% of its value.

How do you get Cool Cucumber?

Cool Cucumber comes from: Blob Road (1 in 61), Sky Road (3.9%), Rainbow Road (5.2%), Galaxy Road (6.5%), Basic Egg (1 in 55) or Golden Egg (7.5%).

How much money does Cool Cucumber make?

$65 per second on your base, before your own boosts (rebirths, base upgrades, friends, passes). A Galaxy ★★★ Cool Cucumber makes $9.75K per second.
