How seasons work

  • Each season brings its own LIMITED rarity, a season event and a new look for the lobby. It switches on and off by itself, even in servers that are already running.
  • Limited blobs only spawn on the Blob Road while their season runs, and much more often during the season event.
  • You keep them forever. After the season they're trade-only, so they're worth holding on to (trade calculator).
  • A new blob also drops every Friday, outside the seasons (updates).

Questions

When does the Winter season start in Steal a Blob?

Wednesday, November 4, 2026 at 00:00 UTC. It runs until January 7, 2027.

When does the Halloween season end?

Wednesday, November 4, 2026 at 00:00 UTC.

Do limited blobs come back?

No. LIMITED blobs only spawn during their season. You keep the ones you have, and you can still trade for them.

How do I get limited blobs?

Buy them off the Blob Road while their season runs. They're much more common during the season's own event.
