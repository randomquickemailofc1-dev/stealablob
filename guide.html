How to Play Steal a Blob

Everything a new player needs: buying blobs, getting rich, protecting your base, stealing, merging and rebirths. Every number comes straight from the game.

Getting started

  • You start with $100 and a free Puddle Blob.
  • Blobs walk along the Blob Road. Hold the button on one to buy it: it walks to your base and earns cash every second.
  • Step on the green plate in front of a blob to collect its cash.
  • Rarer blobs earn way more: Puddle Blob makes $1/s, Dragon Drip makes $4.3K/s. Keep swapping weak blobs for better ones (see the value list).
  • Your base has 8 spots. Every rebirth adds 2 more, up to 32 over three floors. Extra blobs go to your backpack (60 spots) and don't earn until you place them.
  • Your blobs keep earning while you're offline: 35% of your income for up to 12 hours.
  • Brand new? The beginner's guide walks through every first-day goal in order, with its reward.

Get rich fast

  • Merge twins: two identical blobs become one ★★ blob that earns x2.5; two ★★ make a ★★★ at x6.
  • Play with friends: +10% income for every friend in your server (up to +50%).
  • Join the Roblox group: +10% income forever.
  • Be there on the hour: the first 5 minutes of every hour are ⚡ Golden Hour (2x CASH for 5 minutes) or 🌧️ Blob Rain (Blobs spawn 3x faster with 1.5x luck).
  • Blob Party every Saturday at 16:00 UTC for 1 hour: 3x luck, 3x mutations, 2x cash and 2x spawns.
  • Free stuff: codes, the daily reward (day 7 is a free blob: 10% Mythic, 35% Legendary, otherwise Epic), a free spin every 6 hours, and daily and weekly quests.
  • Upgrade your base: Golden Pedestals gives +3% income for all your blobs per level.
  • Fill your Index: finding every blob of a rarity gives +10% income forever.
  • Rebirth when you can afford it: +50% income forever, every time (below).

Protect your base

  • Press LOCK BASE to close your base for 60 seconds. Do it before you walk away. Locked bases can't be entered.
  • Make the lock last longer: +5 s per rebirth (up to +60 s); Steel Doors in the Rebirth shop (+15s base lock per level); Reinforced Lock base upgrade (+3 s base lock per level); the Long Lock pass (90 s).
  • When you join a server, your base is locked for 90 seconds.
  • When the alarm goes off, tap 🧤 SLAP on the alarm card and chase the red arrow. Slap the thief: they drop your blob and it flies back home. Far away? Tap 🏠 GO HOME.
  • Steal Guard (gems): thieves need 5% longer to steal your blobs per level.
  • The richest player in the server has a bounty: more income, but everyone wants to rob you.

Stealing

  • Walk into someone's base, hold the prompt on a blob and run it back to your base.
  • Holding the prompt takes longer for rarer blobs: 1.5 s for Common up to 8 s for Divine. Every blob page lists its steal time.
  • You walk slower while carrying a blob (speed 12.5 instead of 16; the Fast Carry pass keeps you at 16). If someone slaps you, you drop it and it flies home.
  • If you don't get it home within 2 minutes, it goes back.
  • After a steal you're WANTED: anyone who slaps you gets a bounty.
  • Got robbed? Steal back from the thief within 5 minutes for a REVENGE bonus.
  • Quiet server? With 3 players or fewer, Blob Bandits set up bases you can rob (up to 12 steals a day).

Merging

  • Put two identical blobs on your base (same blob, mutation and stars) and hold MERGE on one of them.
  • ★★ earns and is worth x2.5, ★★★ x6. Stars stack with mutations: a Galaxy ★★★ blob is worth x150.
  • Base full? Extra blobs go to your backpack. Sell (you get 50% of the value back) or merge to make room.

Rebirths

  • A rebirth resets your cash and blobs but gives +50% income forever, 2 more base spots and tokens for the Rebirth shop: Golden Touch (+10% income per level), Steel Doors (+15s base lock per level), Night Shift (+10% offline earnings per level), Lucky Hands (Free spin 1 hour sooner per level).
  • Exclusive blobs come with you through a rebirth. You can rebirth up to 25 times.
  • Rebirths unlock rarer blobs (Celestial at rebirth 1, Galactic at rebirth 3), the sky islands: Sky Island (Rebirth 2+), Rainbow Isle (Rebirth 4+), Galaxy Isle (Rebirth 7+), and the worlds below.

Too easy? Hard mode

  • From Rebirth 2, a 🦹 bandit thief that gets home keeps your blob in its hideout. Only you can steal it back, within 5 minutes, or it's gone. Lock your base and keep your best blobs upstairs.
  • 🔥 HARD challenges (📜 QUESTS → 🔥 HARD, from Rebirth 2): 2 tough challenges a day, +10 💎 and 15 minutes of income each, +15 💎 and a Golden Egg for both. Finish 10 for the 🔥 Daredevil title.
  • 😈 Heist Mode (opt-in from level 5, in the 🔥 HARD tab): about twice the thieves, they keep blobs at any rebirth and bosses get angry. In return: +25% income while thieves can reach your base, and 2x cash for slapping thieves on it. Turned off, it can go back on after 10 minutes.

Eggs and mutations

  • Eggs at the lobby egg stall and in every world hatch random blobs; each egg shows its odds. The Lucky Meter guarantees the egg's top prize after enough hatches. All odds: eggs.
  • Blobs from the road and from eggs can roll a mutation that multiplies income and value, from Silver (x1.25) to Galaxy (x25, 1 in 500). See every mutation and its real odds.

Events

  • Every 15 minutes a random server event runs for 3 minutes: Blob Frenzy (Blobs spawn twice as fast), Lucky Rush (2x luck for rare blobs), Mutation Storm (3x mutation chance (6x ELECTRIC)), Spooky Night (LIMITED Halloween blobs on the road + extra SHADOW and GHOST mutations).
  • The first 5 minutes of every hour: ⚡ Golden Hour (2x CASH for 5 minutes) or 🌧️ Blob Rain (Blobs spawn 3x faster with 1.5x luck).
  • Blob Party every Saturday at 16:00 UTC: the biggest boosts of the week, and a rare blob drops on the road every 5 minutes.
  • Galaxy Hour every Sunday at 17:00 UTC for 1 hour: the Galaxy mutation is 5x likelier on every road and from every egg.
  • Mutation of the Day: every day one mutation is 2x likelier (the home page shows which one, today and tomorrow).
  • Friend boost: play with a Roblox friend in the same server for 5 min and you both get 2x Galaxy on your egg hatches for 30 min, once a day per friend.
  • Blob Friday: a brand new blob every Friday (see updates). Seasons bring LIMITED blobs that never come back.

  • When a warning on the ground fills up, the hit lands: jump slams and step out of the circles. Gems buy stronger slaps, which hit bosses harder.

Trading

  • Open TRADE and pick a player: up to 4 blobs per side, and both sides must press READY.
  • From level 8 you can visit the Trading Plaza to find more traders and claim a booth.
  • Check a trade before you press READY with the trade calculator: trades can't be undone.
  • Never trade Robux, accounts or items outside the game. That's how scams work.

Questions

Is Steal a Blob free?

Yes. It's a free Roblox game; game passes and Robux eggs are optional.

How do you get money fast in Steal a Blob?

Keep swapping your weakest blobs for better ones, merge identical blobs (★★ = x2.5, ★★★ = x6), play during Golden Hour and the Blob Party, play with friends (+10% income each) and rebirth as soon as you can (+50% income forever).

How do you protect your base?

Press LOCK BASE before you walk away (60 seconds, longer with rebirths and upgrades), and slap thieves when the alarm goes off: they drop your blob.

What's the best blob in Steal a Blob?

The blob that earns the most per second is Pharaoh Gloop Jr ($4.2T/s). See the tier list for every blob ranked.
