⏰ Hourly events

The first 5 min of every other hour (UTC), in every server.

  • ⚡ Golden Hour: 2x CASH for 5 minutes! Next one: soon.
  • 🌧️ Blob Rain: Blobs spawn 3x faster with 1.5x luck! Next one: soon.

🎲 Random events

Every 15 minutes a server rolls a random event for 3 minutes. Better blobs reach the road while it runs.

  • Blob Frenzy: Blobs spawn twice as fast!
  • Lucky Rush: 2x luck for rare blobs!
  • Mutation Storm: 3x mutation chance (6x ELECTRIC)!
  • Spooky Night: LIMITED Halloween blobs on the road + extra SHADOW and GHOST mutations!

📦 Supply drops

Every 8 to 12 minutes a crate parachutes down next to the Blob Road, under a gold light beam.

  • Everyone who can open it hears "landing in 20 seconds". Follow the beam.
  • Hold OPEN for 5 seconds. The first player to finish wins. A slap knocks people out of reach, so expect a fight.
  • The prize is one of: 10 💎 gems (30%), 5 minutes of your income (at least $5K) (27%), 5 minutes of 2x cash (18%), a free Golden Egg (15%), a Potion to drink later (5%), a Potion to drink later (5%).
  • From level 3. An unopened crate disappears after 3 minutes.

🌑 Blackouts

Every 25 to 40 minutes (with at least 2 players it can hit), the power fails.

  • 15 seconds of warning, then every lock is down for 60 seconds: player bases and bandit hideouts. Rob anyone, or stand guard and slap.
  • Your lock isn't wasted: it pauses during the blackout and picks up again when the power's back.
  • Never hits players below level 5, brand-new players, or anyone in their first 2 minutes in a server.

🦹 Bandits and raids

  • Bandit hideouts move into empty plots while a server has 3 players or fewer (up to 2 at once), so there is always someone to rob.
  • Their lasers switch off and on: wait for OFF, grab a blob and run home. 90 seconds between bandit steals, up to 12 a day.
  • Bandit thieves raid YOUR unlocked base about every 2.5 to 4 minutes while you're near it (more often after a few rebirths). 15% of raids are two thieves at once. Slap them for cash, or lock your base.

😈 Kidnaps

  • From Rebirth 2, a bandit thief that gets home keeps your blob for 5 minutes.
  • Only you can steal it back from the hideout (a red countdown shows over it). If the time runs out, it's gone.
  • Locking your base, or slapping thieves before they get out, stops it.
  • At most one kidnap every 30 minutes, and never while you stand still (AFK): then the blob just hops home.

🆕 Blob Friday

  • A new blob joins the Blob Road every Friday at 16:00 UTC.
  • Next drop in Oct 30. The home page shows what's new.

🏆 Weekend Steal Contest

Starts with Blob Friday and runs until Monday at 00:00 UTC. Next: this weekend.

  • Every blob you steal and get home scores: 2 points from another player's base, 1 from a bandit base.
  • At most 5 steals from the same player count in one weekend.
  • The top 3 get a limited title with the weekend's date. The live top 5 shows in game.

🎉 Blob Party

Every Saturday at 16:00 UTC for 1 hour. Next: this week.

  • x2 cash, x3 luck, x3 mutations and x2 spawns in every server.
  • Every 5 minutes a party blob drops on the road (Mythic, Secret, Divine).

🌌 Galaxy Hour

Every Sunday at 17:00 UTC for 1 hour. Next: this week.

  • The Galaxy mutation is x5 as likely everywhere. All the odds: Galaxy guide.

👑 Play with the dev

Every Saturday at 16:00 UTC for 1 hour. Next: this week.

  • The developer plays in a public server. Whenever a dev is playing, a 👑 JOIN card shows up in game so you can hop into their server.
  • Players in the dev's server get the 🏅 Met the Dev badge.

🌍 Community goal

  • Every steal in every server, Monday to Saturday, counts toward one shared weekly goal (you can see it in game).
  • Reached: x2 luck in every server all weekend, from Saturday at 00:00 UTC to Monday at 00:00 UTC.

Questions

When is the next Blob Party in Steal a Blob?

Every Saturday at 16:00 UTC for 1 hour.

How often do supply drops come?

Every 8 to 12 minutes in each server. Hold OPEN for 5 seconds to win the crate.

What happens in a blackout?

Every lock in the server goes down for 60 seconds after a 15-second warning. New and low-level players are safe.
