How the verdict works

  • Each blob's value is the game's formula: its price × its mutation × its stars. Up to 4 blobs per side, like in the game.
  • W (win): what you get is worth more than 10% more than what you give. L (loss): it's worth more than 10% less. F (fair): anything in between.
  • Income per second is shown too: a trade can lose value but still earn you more, or the other way round.
  • It can't know what blobs people want most right now, so a fair trade on paper can still be a bad deal if nobody wants what you get.
  • Trades can't be undone. Never trade Robux, accounts or items outside the game: that's how scams work.
