What you get

  • 🎟️

    Your own creator code

    Your viewers type it in the game's CODES window and get 10 💎 + a FREE Golden Egg. Every use is credited to you, so you can see how many players your videos bring.

  • 🎥

    Creator title + tag

    A 🎥 CREATOR tag over your head and in chat, and the 🎥 Creator title in the game. Players will know who you are.

  • 📣

    Shoutouts

    We share your videos in our Discord and on our TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

  • 👀

    Sneak peeks

    See the next Blob Friday blob and big updates before anyone else, so your video can be first.

  • 🔗

    Your tracking link

    stealablob.com/?ref=yt-yourname: it only counts how many players come from you (no personal data).

  • 💰

    Later: Robux rewards (planned)

    Not a promise yet: once the program grows we want to reward the creators who bring the most players. We'll tell you before anything starts.

How it works

  1. ApplyFill in the form below. Join our Discord too, so we can message you.
  2. We check your channelUsually within a few days. Any size is fine; we care that you post regularly and keep it friendly.
  3. Get your code + linkPut both in your video description or pinned comment.
  4. Make videos!Play, steal, merge, hunt mutations. Tell us when a video is up and we'll share it.

Video ideas

  • Noob to Rebirth 1: a fresh account gets there in about 20 minutes.
  • Hunting a Galaxy blob: the rarest mutation, 1 in 500 on the road (better in Galaxy Hour). See the Galaxy guide.
  • Stealing from the richest base (and getting slapped trying).
  • The Weekend Steal Contest: Friday to Sunday, the top 3 thieves win a title.
  • Blob Friday: a new blob every Friday. Be the first to show it.
  • Opening 100 eggs: the egg simulator shows the real odds first.

The few rules

  • Be honest: no fake “free Robux”, admin or hack claims.
  • No exploits or cheats in videos.
  • Keep it friendly: lots of our players are young.
  • Say what you really think. Good or bad, honest videos are fine.
  • Heads-up: the game is currently open to players 16+ on Roblox. Mention it so younger viewers aren't disappointed.

No. Any size is fine. We like small channels that post regularly.

Ask us in Discord: we can tell you how many players used your code and came through your link.
