What is Steal a Blob?

A free Roblox game about blobs: squishy collectible creatures that walk along the Blob Road. Buy them, let them earn on your base, steal the rare ones from other players and keep yours safe.

  • 🛣️

    Buy blobs off the road

    69 blobs in 12 rarities, from Common to Galactic. 45 of them walk along the Blob Road: hold the button on one and it walks to your base. The rest hatch from eggs or drop from bosses.

  • 🏠

    Build the richest base

    Your blobs earn cash every second. 8 spots to start, up to 32 over three floors, plus base upgrades.

  • 🦹

    Steal and defend

    Sneak into other bases and run blobs home. Lock your base, slap thieves to make them drop your blob, and steal back for a revenge bonus.

  • ✨

    9 mutations

    Blobs can spawn Silver, Gold, Rainbow, Galaxy and more, up to x25 value.

  • ⭐

    Merge for stars

    Two identical blobs make one with an extra star: up to ★★★ for x6.

  • ♻️

    Rebirths and sky islands

    Rebirth up to 25 times for +50% income each, and unlock 3 sky islands with luckier roads.

  • 🌋

    Worlds and bosses

    4 worlds (Candy Cove, Lava Land, Frost Peaks, Cosmic Rift), each with its own egg and a boss you slap together with other players.

  • 🤝

    Trading

    Trade up to 4 blobs a side, or visit the Trading Plaza from level 8.

  • 🆕

    A new blob every Friday

    Blob Friday adds a blob every week, and seasons bring LIMITED blobs.

  • 🎉

    Weekly events

    The Blob Party, Galaxy Hour, hourly Golden Hour and Blob Rain, and a Mutation of the Day.
