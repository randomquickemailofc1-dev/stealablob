Get your own page

  • In Steal a Blob, open SETTINGS and turn on 🌐 Public player page. It's off until you turn it on.
  • Your page appears at stealablob.com/players/<your username> within a day (the site updates daily) and follows your base from then on.
  • It shows your display name and username, base value, best blobs, level, rebirths and the title you wear. Nothing else.
  • Changed your mind? Turn it off and the page is removed at the next update.
