Your first day

One goal at a time, on the card at the top of your screen (with a beam that points the way). Each goal pays out when you claim it, plus 25 XP. The times are what a normal player needs from their first join. Big rewards have a gold border.

  1. 1

    Goal 1: Buy your first blob on the Blob Road

    Blobs ride along the Blob Road. Hold the button on one you can afford and it walks to your base.

    • 💵30 s of income (at least $150)
    ~2 min
  2. 2

    Goal 2: Step on a green plate to collect cash

    Each blob drops its cash on the green plate in front of it. Step on the plate to pick it up.

    • 💵30 s of income (at least $200)
    ~2 min
  3. 3

    Goal 3: Steal a blob (try a 🦹 Bandit base!)

    Walk into another base, hold the prompt on a blob and run it home. 🦹 Bandit bases are the easy first target: grab a blob while their lasers are OFF.

    • 💵60 s of income (at least $500)
    • 💎3 gems
    • 🧤the Pickpocket title
    ~2 min
  4. 4

    Goal 4: Own 5 blobs

    Your base has 8 spots. Keep buying: cheap blobs off the road add up fast.

    • 💵60 s of income (at least $400)
    ~3 min
  5. 5

    Goal 5: Earn $250/s

    Swap your weakest blob for a better one, merge twins and collect often. More tricks: get rich fast.

    • 🥚a free Basic Egg
    ~5 min
  6. 6

    Goal 6: Upgrade your base to Level 1

    Open MY BASE and upgrade it. Level 1 costs $25K and makes every blob on your base earn more.

    • 💵60 s of income (at least $2.5K)
    • 💎5 gems
    ~6 min
  7. 7

    Goal 7: Merge two twin blobs into a ⭐⭐ blob

    Put two identical blobs (same blob, mutation and stars) on your base and hold MERGE on one of them.

    • 💎10 gems
    ~8 min
  8. 8

    Goal 8: Earn $1K/s

    Swap your weakest blob for a better one, merge twins and collect often. More tricks: get rich fast.

    • 💵2 min of income (at least $5K)
    ~11 min
  9. 9

    Goal 9: Steal 5 blobs

    Bandit hideouts and unlocked bases are fair game. Only blobs you get home count.

    • 🥚a free Golden Egg
    ~14 min
  10. 10

    Goal 10: Rebirth for the first time

    Rebirth 1 costs $1.5M. You start over with +50% income forever, 2 more base spots and rebirth tokens.

    • 💎15 gems
    • ⚡2x cash for 10 min
    • 🔁the Reborn title
    ~18 min
  11. 11

    Goal 11: Upgrade your base to Level 2

    Open MY BASE and upgrade it. Level 2 costs $200K and makes every blob on your base earn more.

    • 💎10 gems
    ~22 min
  12. 12

    Goal 12: Unlock 🍭 Candy Cove (🌍 WORLDS)

    Open 🌍 WORLDS. Candy Cove needs level 3 and $100K. What's there.

    • 💎10 gems
    ~22 min
  13. 13

    Goal 13: Defeat the Sugar King

    Slap the Sugar King together with everyone in the arena (60 health, +40 per extra player). Everyone who helps gets the reward. Its moves.

    • 💎10 gems
    ~26 min
  14. 14

    Goal 14: Play for 30 minutes

    Nothing special to do: every minute in game counts.

    • 🥚a free Golden Egg
    • 🌅the Day One title
    ~30 min
  15. 15

    Goal 15: Rebirth 2 times

    Rebirth 2 costs $6.75M. You start over with +50% income forever, 2 more base spots and rebirth tokens.

    • 💎15 gems
    ~31 min
  16. 16

    Goal 16: Fly up to ☁️ Sky Island (Rebirth 2)

    From Rebirth 2, fly up to Sky Island: its own Blob Road with 3x luck.

    • 💎10 gems
    ~32 min
  17. 17

    Goal 17: Earn $5K/s

    Swap your weakest blob for a better one, merge twins and collect often. More tricks: get rich fast.

    • 🥚a free Cosmic Egg
    ~39 min

Come back tomorrow

On your 2nd, 3rd and 7th day of playing (they don't have to be in a row) a short set of goals shows up once the first-day goals are done. Each set pays by itself when it's finished.

  • 🌄 Play day 2

    • Steal 2 blobs
    • Hatch or merge 1 time
    • Earn 8 min of your income (at least $5K)
    • 🥚a free Golden Egg
    • 🌄the Day Two title

  • 🗓️ Play day 3

    • Hatch or merge 2 times
    • Earn 5 min of your income (at least $5K)
    • 💎5 gems
    • 🥚a free Golden Egg

  • 🏅 Play day 7

    • Steal 3 blobs
    • Earn 6 min of your income (at least $5K)
    • 🥚a free Cosmic Egg
    • 🏅the Regular title

Tips for new players

  • Lock your base before you walk away: LOCK BASE closes it for 60 seconds.
  • Slap thieves when the alarm goes off: they drop your blob and it flies home.
  • Play with friends: +10% income for every friend in your server.
  • Join the Roblox group for +10% income forever.
  • Redeem the codes once you have a few blobs: most pay minutes of your income, so they're worth more later.
  • Be there on the hour: the first 5 minutes of every hour are ⚡ Golden Hour or 🌧️ Blob Rain.
  • Blob Party every Saturday at 16:00 UTC: 3x luck and 2x cash for an hour.

Questions

What should you do first in Steal a Blob?

Follow the objective card at the top of your screen. The tutorial walks you through buy your first blob on the blob road, step on a green plate to collect cash and steal a blob, then the game hands you 14 more first-day goals, one at a time.

How long does the first rebirth take in Steal a Blob?

About 18 minutes for a normal player following the goals. Rebirth 1 costs $1.5M. It gives +50% income forever.

What do you get for playing 30 minutes on your first day?

The "Play for 30 minutes" goal pays a free Golden Egg and the Day One title.

What if I skip a goal?

Nothing is lost. If you're already past a goal you haven't done, the game skips it and moves on; do it later and you can still claim it.
