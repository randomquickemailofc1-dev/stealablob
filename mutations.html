The Mutation Machine

  • Next to the Blob Factory. Pick one of your blobs, pay 💎 gems and it rolls a new mutation. A reroll never lands on no mutation.
  • You choose: KEEP the old mutation or TAKE the new one. If you don't pick within 60 seconds (or you leave), the better one is kept for you.
  • Pity: the 10th reroll of the same kind of blob since its last Diamond-or-better roll is guaranteed Diamond or better.
  • Its odds never change: luck, events, Galaxy Hour and the Mutation of the Day don't affect it. Up to 30 rerolls a day. The game shows these odds before you pay; where paid random items aren't allowed, the machine is off.

Questions

What is the rarest mutation in Steal a Blob?

Galaxy: x25 value and income, 1 in 500 on the Blob Road.

How many mutations are there in Steal a Blob?

9: Silver (x1.25), Gold (x1.5), Neon (x2), Diamond (x3), Frozen (x4), Molten (x5), Shadow (x7), Rainbow (x10), Galaxy (x25).

How do mutations work?

Every blob that spawns on a road or hatches from an egg rolls for a mutation. A mutation multiplies the blob's value and income, and stacks with stars from merging.

Can you change a blob's mutation?

Yes, with the Mutation Machine: it rerolls the mutation for gems and you choose to keep the old one or take the new one.
