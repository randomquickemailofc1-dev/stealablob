Getting started

What is Steal a Blob?

A free Roblox game by !Devx Games: buy blobs off the Blob Road, build the richest base, and steal the rarest blobs from other players' bases while you protect your own.

Does Steal a Blob cost anything?

No. The game is free; game passes and Robux eggs are optional extras.

Who can play Steal a Blob?

Currently open to players 16+. You need a Roblox account; it runs wherever Roblox does.

What should a new player do first?

Follow the goals on the objective card: buy a blob, collect its cash, make your first steal. The beginner's guide lists every first-day goal.

Money and rebirths

What's the fastest way to earn cash in Steal a Blob?

Keep swapping weak blobs for better ones, merge twins (★★ = x2.5, ★★★ = x6), play with friends (+10% each) and rebirth when you can (+50% income forever). Every boost: get rich fast.

How does rebirth work?

A rebirth resets your cash and blobs but gives you a permanent income boost, more base spots and rebirth tokens. The first one costs $1.5M and you can rebirth up to 25 times.

Do blobs earn money while I'm offline?

Yes: 35% of your income for up to 12 hours, waiting for you when you come back.

Stealing and defending

How do you steal a blob?

Walk into another player's base (or a 🦹 bandit hideout), hold the prompt on a blob and carry it back to your own base. If someone slaps you on the way, you drop it.

How do you stop people stealing your blobs?

Press LOCK BASE (60 seconds, longer with rebirths and upgrades) and slap thieves when the alarm goes off: they drop your blob and it flies home.

What happens if a bandit steals my blob?

Before Rebirth 2 it just hops home. From Rebirth 2, a bandit thief that gets away keeps it in its hideout for 5 minutes: steal it back in time or it's gone.

Blobs, mutations and merging

How many blobs are in Steal a Blob?

89 right now, and a new one drops every Friday at 16:00 UTC. All of them: the value list.

What is the most valuable blob?

Pharaoh Gloop Jr (Divine), worth $400T before mutations and stars. See it on its page.

How do mutations work?

Every blob from the road or an egg can roll a mutation that multiplies its value and income, from Silver (x1.25) to Galaxy (x25, 1 in 500). Odds everywhere: mutations.

How do you merge blobs?

Put two identical blobs (same blob, mutation and stars) on your base and hold MERGE: they become one blob with an extra star, worth x2.5 at ★★ and x6 at ★★★.

Worlds and bosses

How do you unlock new worlds?

Open 🌍 WORLDS: each world needs a level, sometimes rebirths, and a one-time fee. Candy Cove: level 3 and $100K, Lava Land: level 8, 1 rebirth and $5M and Frost Peaks: level 15, 3 rebirths and $250M. All of them: worlds.

How do you beat a boss?

Slap it together with everyone in the arena and dodge its moves (step out of the glowing areas before they fill up). Stronger gloves do more damage: gloves.

Codes and free stuff

Where can I find Steal a Blob codes?

On the codes page (8 working right now), plus a new code every Monday in our Discord.

How do you get gems?

World bosses pay gems to everyone who helps. Journey goals, the daily reward and supply drops give some too.

Events

When is the Blob Party?

Every Saturday at 16:00 UTC for 1 hour: 3x luck, 3x mutations and 2x cash. This week's events.

What is Galaxy Hour?

Every Sunday at 17:00 UTC, the Galaxy mutation is 5x as likely for an hour. More: Galaxy guide.

Trading

How do you trade in Steal a Blob?

Open TRADE and pick a player: up to 4 blobs a side, and both must press READY. From level 8 the Trading Plaza has more traders.

How do I know if a trade is fair?

Put both sides in the trade calculator before you press READY: trades can't be undone. Never trade Robux, accounts or items outside the game.
