How this is ranked

  • By income per second: the cash a blob makes on your base, the thing that gets you rich. A blob's value in Steal a Blob is always 25 to 125 seconds of its income, so ranking by value gives almost the same list.
  • S is the top 10% of all 69 blobs, A the next 20%, B the next 30%, C the next 25% and D the rest. Blobs that earn the same are always in the same tier.
  • Mutations and stars don't change the order: they multiply every blob the same way, so a Rainbow ★★★ blob stays in its tier.
  • Tags show how hard a blob is to get (Limited, Boss drop, Robux egg, Rebirth needed). They don't move a blob up: a hard-to-get blob that earns less is still worse on your base. For trades, check the trade calculator.
  • Rebuilt from the game files with every update, so new blobs slot in by themselves.
