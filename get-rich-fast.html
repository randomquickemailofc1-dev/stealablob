How to Get Rich Fast

Every way to make more cash in Steal a Blob, with the game's real numbers: how your income is worked out, the best blobs to buy, merging, rebirths, timed cash boosts and free cash.

The short version

  1. 1
    Fill every spot with the best blobs you can afford. Your base has 8 spots to start, so a spot holding a cheap blob is wasted money. Swap up whenever you can.
  2. 2
    Merge twins. Two identical blobs make one ★★ blob that earns x2.5: more cash from one spot instead of two.
  3. 3
    Rebirth as soon as you can. +50% income forever, every time, plus 2 more spots.
  4. 4
    Play when cash is doubled: Golden Hour (first 5 minutes of every other hour) and the Blob Party (weekly).
  5. 5
    Bring friends: +10% income per friend in your server.
  6. 6
    Grab the free cash: 8 codes, daily rewards, the free spin and quests.

How your income is worked out

Straight from the game's code: each blob's income per second (see the value list) times your multiplier:

blob income × rebirths × cash boosts × (1 + bonuses)

  • Rebirths: x(1 + 0.5 per rebirth). 1 rebirth = x1.5, 2 = x2, 4 = x3.
  • Cash boosts multiply on top: Golden Hour x2, the Blob Party x2, 2x cash boosts (the welcome-back gift and the Starter Pack give one) and the 2x Cash game pass.
  • Bonuses add up first, then multiply:
    • +10% per friend in your server (up to +50%)
    • +10% for joining the Roblox group
    • +10% per rarity you've fully collected in your Index
    • +8% per Base Level (bought with cash, kept through rebirths; level 1 costs $25K)
    • +3% per Golden Pedestals level (10 levels, first one $15K)
    • +10% per Golden Touch level in the Rebirth shop (up to 5)
    • +10% per Exclusive blob on your base (up to +50%)
    • +10% while you're the richest player in the server (you get a bounty on your head)
    • +20% with the VIP game pass

Example: with 2 rebirths, 3 friends in your server and the group joined, every blob earns x2.8 (x2 × 1.4); during Golden Hour that doubles to x5.6.

Merging

  • Two identical blobs (same blob, mutation and stars) merge into one with a star more. ★★ earns and is worth x2.5, ★★★ x6.
  • Two ★ blobs in two spots earn x2 together; merged, they earn x2.5 from one spot, and the free spot takes another blob.
  • Four ★ blobs become one ★★★ blob earning x6, in one spot.
  • Stars multiply with mutations: see all mutations.

Rebirths

A rebirth resets your cash and blobs (Exclusive blobs stay), but the bonus is forever. You can rebirth up to 25 times. Rebirths also unlock the sky islands (Sky Island at 2, Rainbow Isle at 4, Galaxy Isle at 7) and Celestial blobs at 1, Galactic blobs at 3.

Cash boosts: when to play

  • ⚡ Golden Hour: x2 cash for the first 5 minutes of every other hour (even hours UTC). Next one: soon.
  • 🎉 Blob Party every Saturday at 16:00 UTC for 1 hour: x2 cash, x3 luck, x3 mutations and x2 spawns. Next one: this week.
  • Random server events every 15 minutes for 3 minutes (like Lucky Rush and Blob Frenzy) bring better blobs to the road faster.

Free cash

  • Codes: 8 working codes. Each gives the listed cash or minutes of your income, whichever is more, so they're worth more once you're rich.
  • Daily rewards for 7 days in a row: $500, $2K, $5K, $15K, $40K, $100K (or minutes of your income if that's more), then a free blob on day 7.
  • Free spin every 6 hours, plus daily and weekly quests.
  • Mutation Index: the first milestone (5 blob × mutation finds) pays $5K or 5 min of your income, later ones pay eggs and gems (all goals).
  • Offline: your blobs keep earning 35% of your income for up to 12 hours: raise it with Night Shift (Rebirth shop, +10% offline earnings per level, up to 4 levels) and Sweet Dreams (base upgrade, +5% offline earnings rate per level, 3 levels).

Stealing (and not getting robbed)

  • Rarer blobs take longer to grab (1.5 s for Common up to 8 s for Divine), you walk slower while carrying one, and a slap makes you drop it: pick targets you can get home.
  • Revenge: steal back from your robber within 5 minutes for an extra 50% of the blob's value.
  • Quiet server? With 3 players or fewer, Blob Bandits set up bases you can rob (up to 12 steals a day).
  • Lock your base before you walk away (LOCK BASE, 60 seconds). More in the beginner guide.

Questions

What's the fastest way to make money in Steal a Blob?

Fill every base spot with the priciest blobs you can afford, merge identical blobs (★★ earns x2.5), rebirth as soon as you can (+50% income forever each time), play with friends (+10% each) and be online for Golden Hour (2x cash) and the Blob Party.

When should I rebirth in Steal a Blob?

As soon as you can afford it. A rebirth resets your cash and blobs, but every rebirth adds +50% income forever and 2 base spots. The first one costs $1.5M.

Do I earn money while offline?

Yes: 35% of your income for up to 12 hours, more with upgrades.

How much do friends boost income?

+10% income for every friend in your server, up to +50%.
