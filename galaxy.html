Galaxy Hour

  • Every Sunday at 17:00 UTC for 1 hour. Next one: this Sunday.
  • Galaxy is x5 likelier on every road and from every egg: 1 in 500 → 1 in 100 on the Blob Road.
  • Every server gets a warning 30 minutes before it starts.

The friend boost

  • Play in the same server as a Roblox friend for 5 min in a row and you both get x2 Galaxy on your egg hatches for 30 min.
  • Once a day per pair of friends, up to 2 times a day per player. It stacks with Galaxy Hour (up to the 1 in 20 cap).

The Mutation Machine

  • Reroll a blob's mutation for gems at the Mutation Machine (next to the Blob Factory): Galaxy is 1 in 500 per reroll, on the 10th pity roll too, and no boost changes that.
  • You always choose to KEEP the old mutation or TAKE the new one, so a reroll never costs you a Galaxy you already have. Odds and costs: Mutation Machine.

Questions

How do you get a Galaxy blob in Steal a Blob?

Any blob from a road or an egg can roll the Galaxy mutation (1 in 500 normally). The best odds are on the Galaxy Road during Galaxy Hour (1 in 20); the Mutation Machine can also reroll a blob into Galaxy (1 in 500 per reroll).

When is Galaxy Hour in Steal a Blob?

Every Sunday at 17:00 UTC for 1 hour. Galaxy is x5 likelier on every road and egg.

How much is a Galaxy blob worth?

x25 its normal value and income. A Galaxy ★★★ blob is x150.
