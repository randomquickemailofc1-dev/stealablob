How slapping works

  • Slap with the glove you have on (click or tap, one slap every 0.8 s). A slap that hits a thief makes them drop your blob and it flies home.
  • Stronger gloves hit bosses harder and knock players further. You keep every glove you buy and pick one to wear.
  • The Golden Slap costs cash ($250K); the rest cost 💎 gems, which world bosses pay out.
  • Most gloves unlock with a world: unlock the world, beat its boss a few times and the gems for its glove add up.

Questions

What is the best glove in Steal a Blob?

The Prism Slap: 32 boss damage per slap and x2 knockback. It costs 10,000 gems.

How do you get the Golden Slap?

Buy it in the slap shop for $250K. It's the only glove that costs cash and hits 2x as hard as your first one.

How do you get gems for gloves?

Beat world bosses: everyone who helps gets gems. Journey goals, the daily reward and supply drops give some too.
