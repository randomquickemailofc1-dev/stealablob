Mutations

Every blob that spawns on the road or hatches from an egg rolls for a mutation, rarest first. A mutation multiplies both value and income. (Pictured: King Wobble.) Odds in every place, the Mutation Machine and the Mutation Index: mutation guide.

Galaxy boosts multiply but never go past 5% per roll.
